Hindi + English Wedding Wording Examples

A lot of Indian wedding invitations naturally mix Hindi and English — it feels warmer and more familiar than pure English, without being formal Hindi that some younger relatives might not read comfortably.

Common words that carry over naturally

Certain Hindi words are so commonly used in invitations that they work even in an otherwise English sentence: shaadi (wedding), shubh (auspicious), ashirwad (blessings), parivaar (family), and milan (union) are all common choices.

Examples

"With their parivaar's ashirwad, [Name] and [Name] are getting married! Please join us for the shubh occasion on [date]."
"Yeh shaadi hone waali hai! Join us as [Name] and [Name] begin their new journey together on [date]."
"With heartfelt ashirwad from both families, we invite you to celebrate the milan of [Name] and [Name]."

A tip on balance

The most natural-sounding invites use Hindi words as accents within an English sentence, rather than switching entirely between languages mid-sentence. Overdoing the mix can feel forced — a few well-placed words usually land better than a full sentence in each language.
