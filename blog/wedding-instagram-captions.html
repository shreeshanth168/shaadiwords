Best Instagram Captions for Your Wedding

A wedding post deserves a caption that matches the moment — not just "so happy 😍". Here are ideas for the three most-posted wedding moments.

Engagement announcement

"He asked. I cried (happy tears, I promise). Officially engaged! 💍"
"Plot twist: we said yes to forever. Engaged as of today!"

Mehendi day

"Mehendi laga ke rakhna — the countdown has officially begun. 🌿"
"Hands full of henna, heart full of excitement. Mehendi done, wedding next!"

Wedding day

"From two families to one. Married! ❤️"
"Best decision, best day, best person. Officially married!"
"Said 'I do' surrounded by everyone we love. Forever starts now."

A quick tip on hashtags

A short custom hashtag combining both names (like #AnanyaWedsRohan) makes it easy for guests to find and share their own photos from your wedding — worth adding to your caption alongside 2-3 general wedding hashtags for reach.
