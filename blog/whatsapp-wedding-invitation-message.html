How to Write a WhatsApp Wedding Invitation

Printed cards go to close family; WhatsApp is how most people actually spread the word to friends, colleagues, and extended relatives today. A good WhatsApp invite is shorter, warmer, and more conversational than a formal card.

What makes a good WhatsApp invite

  • Keep it short — 2-3 sentences is plenty. This isn't the formal card.
  • Lead with the news, not a formal preamble — people are scanning their chats quickly.
  • Include the essentials: names, date, and either the venue or a note that details will follow.
  • Match your relationship with the recipient — closer friends can get a more casual, personal message than distant relatives.

Examples

"Hi! We're getting married on [date] and would love for you to be there. Details to follow soon — save the date! 💍"
"Guess what — it's happening! [Name] and I are tying the knot on [date] in [city]. Would mean the world to have you there."
"You're invited! Join us as we celebrate [Name] & [Name]'s wedding on [date]. Can't wait to celebrate with you 🎉"

A tip for group forwards

If you're sending this to a family WhatsApp group rather than one person, swap "you" for something slightly more universal, like "everyone" or the family/group name, so it doesn't read like a copy-paste to an individual.
