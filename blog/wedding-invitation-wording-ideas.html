50 Wedding Invitation Wording Ideas

Choosing invitation wording is harder than it looks — too formal and it feels stiff, too casual and it can feel out of place for a family event. Here are proven approaches across three common styles, with real examples for each.

1. Traditional wording

Traditional wording works well for family-heavy weddings where elders are heavily involved in planning. It usually includes both families' names and a formal request for the guest's presence.

"Together with their families, [Name] and [Name] request the honour of your presence as they unite in marriage, surrounded by the blessings of loved ones."
"With hearts full of gratitude, the families of [Name] and [Name] joyfully invite you to celebrate their wedding and shower the couple with your blessings."

2. Modern, minimal wording

Modern wording drops the formal family-name structure in favor of something shorter and more personal — popular for couples who want the focus on themselves rather than a long formal preamble.

"[Name] & [Name] are getting married. Come celebrate with us."
"Two hearts, one promise. Join us as we say 'I do' on [date]."

3. Fun and witty wording

Great for younger, casual crowds or a couple whose personality leans playful — often used for the WhatsApp/social side rather than the formal printed card.

"After years of convincing our families we're serious about this, it's official! Join us as we tie the knot."
"Warning: this wedding comes with unlimited food, music, and happy tears. You're invited."

How to pick the right one

A common approach: use traditional wording for the printed invitation card (which elders and relatives will keep), and modern or witty wording for the WhatsApp message and Instagram caption, which tend to reach a younger crowd.
