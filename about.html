About ShaadiWords

Wedding season in India means a hundred small decisions — and one of the most surprisingly stressful ones is: what do we actually write on the invitation?

ShaadiWords exists to solve exactly that — in seconds, for free.

Type in the couple's names, pick the event and a tone, and ShaadiWords writes your formal invitation wording, a WhatsApp message for friends and family, and an Instagram caption — all matched to the occasion, and optionally blended with Hindi, Telugu, or Tamil phrasing.

We built this because wedding planning already has enough moving parts. The wording shouldn't be one more thing keeping you up at night.

ShaadiWords is a small, independently run project — not a big company, just one person trying to make one part of wedding planning a little easier. It's free to use, and it always will be for the core wording generator.
