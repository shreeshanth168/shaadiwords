Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ShaadiWords ("we," "our," "the site") is a free tool that helps people generate wedding invitation wording, WhatsApp messages, and Instagram captions. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

When you use the generator, the details you type in — names, event type, date, city, and any extra details — are sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate your wording. We do not store this information on our own servers or database; it is used only to generate your result and is not saved by us afterward.

Third-party services we use

  • Google Gemini API — generates the invitation wording, WhatsApp message, and Instagram caption based on what you type in.
  • Vercel — hosts this website.
  • Google AdSense (once approved) — may show ads on this site and use cookies to personalize them. You can control ad personalization through Google Ads Settings.

Cookies

This site may use cookies for basic analytics (to understand how many people visit) and, once AdSense is active, for ad personalization. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Since we don't store your generated content or personal details on our servers, there's no account data to request or delete. If you have questions about data sent to Google's services, you can review Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the site grows (for example, when ads or new features are added). Check back occasionally for updates.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the Contact page.
